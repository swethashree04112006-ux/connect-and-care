Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about transparency, volunteering, and giving with confidence.

How do you verify the orphanages listed on the platform?

Each listed home is checked against its government registration certificate, and our district coordinators conduct physical audits before a home is shown as verified.

Does Connect & Care take a commission or transaction fee on donations?

No. Donations made through a home's UPI ID go straight to that institution's bank account, with zero platform commission.

Can I visit the orphanage in person before contributing?

Yes. Use the Profile button on any home to see its contact details and arrange a visit with the administrator.

How do I get an 80G receipt for income tax exemption?

Registered trusts issue 80G receipts directly for eligible donations. Look for the 80G mention on the home's profile before donating.

How can I volunteer my weekend time or professional skills?

Use the "Volunteer Your Time" or "Offer Professional Services" options above to register your interest. Our coordinators will match you with a nearby home.
